Our Commitment

Listening, Responding & Improving

Flag Frenzy Football Ltd aims to provide children and families with a safe, positive and professionally managed camp experience.

We recognise that concerns or complaints may occasionally arise. We want parents, guardians and others who use our services to feel able to raise genuine concerns and to know that those concerns will be considered fairly.

Complaints can also help us identify where something has gone wrong, where communication could have been clearer or where our services, policies or procedures could be improved.

Raising a genuine complaint will not, by itself, result in a child being treated less favourably or prevented from participating in future Flag Frenzy Football activities.

01

What This Policy Covers

This policy can be used to raise concerns or complaints relating to Flag Frenzy Football Camp, including matters concerning:

  • Camp organisation or administration.
  • Communication with parents or guardians.
  • The delivery of camp activities or services.
  • Staff, coaches or volunteers.
  • Behaviour management.
  • Health, safety or welfare arrangements.
  • Inclusion or reasonable adjustments.
  • Booking, cancellation or refund matters.
  • The application of a Flag Frenzy Football Camp policy or procedure.
  • Another aspect of a child's experience at camp.

Some matters require a different or more urgent process. In particular, safeguarding concerns, emergencies and allegations involving possible criminal behaviour should not be delayed while the ordinary complaints process is followed.

02

Raising a Concern Informally

Where appropriate, we encourage parents and guardians to raise a concern as soon as possible.

Many straightforward concerns can be resolved quickly through a conversation with the Camp Director or appropriate member of the camp team.

For example, a parent may simply need clarification about an activity, communication, booking arrangement or something their child has reported.

You are not required to attempt informal resolution before making a formal complaint, particularly where the matter is serious or you do not feel informal discussion would be appropriate.

03

Making a Formal Complaint

Formal complaints should normally be made in writing so that we have a clear record of the issue being raised.

Complaints can be emailed to:

Email: info@flagfrenzyfootball.co.uk

Subject: Formal Complaint – Flag Frenzy Football Camp

To help us understand and investigate the matter, please provide as much relevant information as reasonably possible, such as:

  • Your name and contact details.
  • The child's name, where relevant.
  • The date or camp session concerned.
  • What happened.
  • The names of people involved, if known and relevant.
  • Any steps already taken to resolve the matter.
  • Any relevant messages, documents or other information.
  • What outcome or resolution you are seeking, where applicable.

A complaint will not automatically be rejected simply because some of this information is unavailable.

04

Our Complaints Process

1

Complaint received

We will record the complaint and identify the issues that need to be considered.

2

Acknowledgement

We aim to acknowledge a formal complaint within 5 working days.

3

Investigation

Relevant information will be considered and we may contact the complainant, staff, volunteers or other relevant people for further information.

4

Outcome

We will provide an outcome explaining our findings and, where appropriate, any action arising from the complaint.

5

Review

If the complainant believes a material issue has not been properly considered, they may request an internal review.

05

Investigation

Complaints will be considered fairly and proportionately based on the information reasonably available.

Depending on the nature of the complaint, an investigation may include:

  • Reviewing the original complaint.
  • Reviewing relevant camp records or communications.
  • Speaking with staff, coaches or volunteers involved.
  • Considering relevant policies or procedures.
  • Reviewing accident, incident or attendance information where relevant.
  • Asking the complainant for clarification or additional information.
  • Considering whether further action or changes are required.

Where a complaint concerns the conduct of a particular person, that person may be given an appropriate opportunity to respond to the issues raised.

We will seek to handle complaints objectively. Where reasonably practicable, a person should not be solely responsible for deciding a formal complaint about their own conduct.

06

Timescales & Our Response

We aim to deal with complaints as promptly as reasonably possible.

We aim to provide the outcome of a formal complaint within 28 calendar days of receiving the complaint.

Straightforward matters may be resolved considerably sooner.

If a particularly complex complaint cannot reasonably be concluded within this period, we will explain the reason for the delay and provide an update on the progress of the investigation.

Our response may include:

  • A summary of the complaint considered.
  • Our findings.
  • Clarification or an explanation where appropriate.
  • An apology where something has gone wrong.
  • Corrective action where appropriate.
  • Changes to procedures, communication, training or practice where appropriate.
  • Information about any further review route available.

There may be circumstances where confidentiality, safeguarding, employment or data-protection considerations mean we cannot share every detail of action taken in relation to another person.

07

Requesting an Internal Review

If you remain dissatisfied after receiving the formal complaint outcome, you may ask us to review the matter.

A review request should explain why you believe the original complaint outcome should be reconsidered.

For example, this may be because:

  • Relevant information was not considered.
  • There is significant new information.
  • The complaints procedure was not followed appropriately.
  • The outcome appears inconsistent with the evidence or relevant policy.

Where reasonably practicable, an internal review will be considered by someone who was not solely responsible for the original decision.

The review is intended to consider whether the complaint was handled fairly and whether the original outcome remains appropriate. It does not necessarily require the entire investigation to be repeated.

08

Safeguarding, Emergencies & Serious Concerns

The ordinary complaints process should not delay action where a child's immediate safety or welfare may be at risk.

If a child is in immediate danger or requires urgent medical assistance, call 999.

Complaints or information that raise a safeguarding concern will be considered in accordance with our Safeguarding Policy and may need to be referred to the appropriate safeguarding, statutory or emergency authority.

Where allegations concern possible criminal behaviour or another matter requiring external investigation, Flag Frenzy Football Ltd may need to limit or adjust its own investigation so that it does not interfere with the work of the appropriate authority.

09

Fair & Respectful Communication

We will aim to communicate respectfully with people raising concerns and ask that communication with our staff and volunteers is also respectful.

Making a complaint, disagreeing with our response or pursuing a complaint firmly will not, by itself, be treated as unreasonable behaviour.

However, threatening, abusive, discriminatory or harassing behaviour towards staff, volunteers, children or other families is not acceptable.

Where communication becomes abusive or seriously disruptive, we may take reasonable steps to manage how further communication is conducted while continuing to consider the substance of any genuine complaint.

10

Complaint Records & Confidentiality

We will keep appropriate records of formal complaints, including the complaint, relevant investigation information, the outcome and any significant action arising from it.

As a minimum operational standard, formal complaint records will normally be retained for at least 3 years from the conclusion of the complaint, unless a longer period is appropriate because of safeguarding, insurance, legal or other legitimate record-keeping requirements.

Complaint information will be handled with appropriate confidentiality and shared only where there is a legitimate reason to do so.

This may include sharing information with relevant staff, insurers, professional advisers, safeguarding bodies, regulators, emergency services or other authorities where appropriate or required.

Personal information is handled in accordance with our Privacy Policy .

11

Consumer Rights & External Advice

This complaints process does not remove or restrict any legal rights or remedies available to a parent, guardian or other consumer.

Where a complaint relates to a paid camp service, applicable consumer rights continue to apply alongside this policy.

Consumers may seek independent advice about their rights from an appropriate consumer advice service.

If Flag Frenzy Football Camp becomes subject to a particular regulator, registration scheme or external complaints route, the relevant information will be made available to parents and guardians where required.

12

Monitoring & Review

Complaints and concerns may be reviewed to identify recurring issues, lessons learned or improvements that could be made to camp services, communication, training, policies or procedures.

This policy was last reviewed in September 2026 and will be reviewed at least annually, or sooner where experience, changes to our services, regulatory requirements or legal obligations indicate that an earlier review is appropriate.

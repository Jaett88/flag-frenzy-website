Getting Started

About the Game

Flag football is a fast-paced, non-contact version of American football. Instead of tackling a player to stop the play, defenders pull a flag from a belt worn around the player's waist. Players develop skills including passing, catching, route-running, defending and teamwork.
Flag Frenzy Football Camp is for children aged 7–15. Players may be grouped by factors such as age, experience and ability during activities to help keep sessions enjoyable and appropriate.
Not at all. Complete beginners are welcome. Coaches will introduce the fundamentals and help children develop their skills throughout camp. Children who already have experience will also have opportunities to build on what they know.
Flag football is a non-contact version of American football. Tackling is not part of the game. Instead, a defender stops the ball carrier by removing a flag from the belt worn around their waist.
Groupings may vary depending on the children attending and the activity being delivered. We aim to group players appropriately by factors such as age, experience and ability while also giving children opportunities to play and work together.

Preparing for Camp

What to Bring

Children should wear comfortable sports clothing suitable for being active and for the weather conditions on the day. Suitable sports trainers should also be worn. Any camp-specific clothing guidance will be provided before attendance.
Children should bring plenty of water, any food or snacks required for the day, suitable clothing for the weather and any necessary personal medical items, such as an inhaler. Flag football equipment required for the sessions will be provided.
Yes. Children should arrive with enough water and food for the time they will be at camp. Specific information about breaks, lunch and what to bring will be included in the information sent to parents before camp.
Please provide all relevant medical, allergy and additional-needs information during registration. If you would like to discuss your child's individual needs before booking, please contact us so we can talk through the support they may require and whether appropriate arrangements can be made.

Camp Information

During Camp

Camp locations may vary. The confirmed venue and full location details for each camp will be provided as part of the relevant camp and booking information.
Camp times will be confirmed for each individual camp. Parents will be given the relevant start, finish, drop-off and collection information before their child attends.
Booking options may vary between camps. Where both individual-day and full-week places are available, the options and prices will be clearly shown when bookings open.
Yes, siblings who fall within the camp's eligible age range can attend the same camp. Depending on their ages and the activities taking place, they may sometimes be placed in different groups during sessions.
Sessions can continue in suitable weather conditions, including light rain. If weather conditions become unsafe, activities may be paused, changed or cancelled as necessary. Parents will be contacted using the details provided during registration if changes affect the camp day.

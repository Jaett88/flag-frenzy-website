About Flag Frenzy Football Camp

Flag Frenzy Football Camp is a fun, high-energy flag football programme for children aged 7–15. We create a positive and inclusive environment where young players can learn new skills, build confidence, make friends and enjoy the game.

Whether a child is trying flag football for the very first time or already has experience playing, our camps are designed to help every player get involved, improve and enjoy being part of the team.

New to the game?

What is Flag Football?

Flag football is a fast-paced, non-contact version of American football. Instead of tackling a player to stop the play, defenders pull a flag from a belt worn around the player's waist.

Players learn many of the core skills used in American football, including passing, catching, running routes, defending and working together as a team — without tackling.

It's an exciting and accessible sport for beginners while still offering plenty of challenge for children who already have experience playing the game.

What We Offer

  • Non-contact flag football for boys and girls
  • Skill-building drills and game-based activities
  • Opportunities to develop passing, catching, route-running and defending skills
  • Team challenges and mini games
  • Confidence-building activities and teamwork
  • Positive and inclusive coaching

Who Can Join?

  • Children aged 7–15
  • Boys and girls
  • Complete beginners — no previous experience needed
  • Children who already play flag football or American football
  • Young people who want to be active, learn new skills and enjoy being part of a team

More Than Football

Our Approach

At Flag Frenzy, we want children to develop more than football skills. Our camps encourage confidence, communication, teamwork, sportsmanship and a positive attitude both on and off the field.

We believe children learn best when they're enjoying themselves, feeling supported and being encouraged to challenge themselves. Our coaching combines skill development with games, competition and plenty of opportunities for every player to get involved.

Most importantly, we want players to leave camp having enjoyed their experience, learned something new and feeling excited to play flag football again.
